How do I get an institutional or bulk enterprise quote? +

You can fill in our Request a Quote form below or click the WhatsApp Quote button directly. Our commercial enterprise team in Coimbatore evaluates institutional volumes (from 5 to 5,000+ units) and delivers custom tier pricing within 2 hours.

Does GEEKDINO TAB 5 support 4G LTE across all Indian telecom providers? +

Yes. GEEKDINO TAB 5 features a dedicated Nano-SIM tray with wideband 4G LTE VoLTE support across Jio, Airtel, Vodafone Idea (Vi), and BSNL networks, ensuring steady connectivity in urban and rural hubs alike.

Can internal storage be expanded for offline educational material? +

Yes. The tablet comes with 128 GB of high-speed onboard memory and includes a dedicated MicroSD (T-Flash) card expansion slot supporting cards up to 512 GB, ideal for storing offline textbooks, video courses, and maps.

What parental controls and school device management options are available? +

GEEKDINO TAB 5 runs clean Android 17 with integrated hardware parental controls. For schools and corporate fleets, we provide pre-configured Kiosk Mode and support standard Mobile Device Management (MDM) platforms to whitelist apps and lock down unauthorized settings.
